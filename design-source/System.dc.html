07 · Motion

  • Hero: 3 images cross-fade every 7s over a slow 18s zoom. Prev/next, dots and pause controls; pauses on hover, focus and hidden tabs.
  • Story strip: a slow 60s marquee with a pause button; it stops on hover and focus.
  • Reveals: 1s rise on entry using CSS view timelines; the timeline fill and active node track scroll.
  • Hovers: 300–400ms colour changes; images zoom to 1.05 over 1.2s.
  • prefers-reduced-motion stops all of it. The main gallery never auto-advances.
